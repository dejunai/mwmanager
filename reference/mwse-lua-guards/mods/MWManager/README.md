MWManager MWSE utilities
=========================

One top-level MWSE mod and one MCM entry. Top-row tab order: mwquickfixes, mwhangwatch, mwinfoloop, mwsoundguard, mwperformers, mwcollide, mwfloatfix, mwstranded, mwguard.

The mwquickfixes tab combines CorruptSaveGuard, DeferredDialogueTeleport, SuppressBodyPartWarnings, SuppressShutdownWarnings, CompanionSneak, and MouseBleedFix. Each has its own toggle and defaults to enabled. CompanionSneak also keeps its creature, escort, and debug options. Its StormAtronach and AttendMe integrations are optional. DeferredDialogueTeleport retains its fade and logging settings. mwinfoloop defaults to enabled; it uses a cache and throttled checks.

mwhangwatch defaults to disabled; its audio watch also defaults to disabled, with a 40 sound-lines-per-second limit when enabled.
